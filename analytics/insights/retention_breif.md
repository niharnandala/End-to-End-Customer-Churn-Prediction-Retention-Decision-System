# Retention Brief
**Telco Customer Churn — Executive Summary**

---

## Situation

The company is losing 26.5% of its customers against an industry benchmark of under 10%. This translates to $139,130 in recurring monthly revenue already gone and a further $104,070 at risk from active customers showing high churn signals.

---

## The Problem

Three things are driving the majority of revenue loss:

Month-to-month Fiber optic customers in their first 10 months churn at 71%. Nearly 3 out of 4 leave. This single segment alone holds $16,266 in recoverable monthly revenue — and the only difference between a customer who stays and one who leaves is a $10/month security add-on.

The first 10 months are the danger window. 46% of all revenue lost comes from customers who never made it past month 10. After month 30 churn drops below 25% and keeps falling. The company is losing customers before they become loyal — not after.

Internal failures are costing more than the competition. $73,704 every month left because of bad support attitudes, network reliability issues, and pricing dissatisfaction. That is 48% more than competitor-driven losses. The biggest threat is not a rival company. It is poor service delivery.

---

## What To Do

**This week** — Contact the 204 active customers on month-to-month Fiber optic contracts in their first 10 months with no security add-on. Offer the security bundle. This single action protects $16,266 in monthly revenue at a cost of $10 per customer per month.

**This month** — Launch a contract upgrade campaign targeting the 728 active month-to-month Fiber optic customers past month 10. A 3-month discount to move them to a one-year contract reduces their churn rate from 44% to 11%.

**This quarter** — Fix internal support and service failures. 991 customers left last period citing fixable internal problems. No retention campaign recovers customers lost to problems the company created itself.

At a conservative 20% retention success rate across all five priority segments, the recoverable monthly revenue is **$49,302 every single month.**
